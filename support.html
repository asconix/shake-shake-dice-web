Frequently asked questions

The dice don’t roll when I shake the phone.

Shake the phone firmly in your hand: the dice rattle while you shake and land when you stop. If shaking doesn’t work, you can always tap the dice button in the lower right corner of the table. Shake detection needs no special permission, but it can be weaker when the phone is in a thick case or is lying on a table.

There is no sound.

Check that the ring/silent switch (or the Action button setting) is not muted and that the volume is turned up. The dice sounds are played by the app itself.

What is free and what needs the unlock?

The classic six-sided die is free forever. The other dice (d4, d8, d10, d12, d20) and the game modes (five-dice hands, colored dice, attack and defend) give you five free rolls each. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks everything. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open any locked mode or dice type to see the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

I bought the unlock, but it still asks me to pay.

Tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID in Settings → your name → Media & Purchases. If it still doesn’t work, write to me and include the order number from Apple’s receipt email.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

Is the roll result really random?

The dice are simulated with real physics, so the result comes from how they tumble, bounce and land on the table.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhones running iOS 16.6 or later. The app is made for iPhone and does not have a separate iPad or Mac version.
