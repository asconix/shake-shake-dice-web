Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

In short: Shake Shake Dice 3D does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It does not contain advertising or third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not send any data about you or your use of the app to me or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Motion sensor. The app reads your iPhone’s motion sensor to detect shaking. This happens only on the device, only while the app is open, and the data is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings and free rolls. The app keeps a few simple values on your device, such as how many free rolls you have used. They never leave your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and the purchase record. I receive no payment details, name or Apple ID. I only see summary sales figures that Apple provides to every developer. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
