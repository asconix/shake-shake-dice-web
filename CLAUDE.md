# Shake Shake Dice website

Static site for the Shake Shake Dice app (GitHub Pages, `.nojekyll`): `index.html`, `marketing.html`, `support.html`, `privacy.html`, `style.css`, icons and screenshots. No build step.

## Rules
- The support and privacy pages are referenced by the App Store listing: don't rename or move them.
- Keep content consistent with the app (`../shake-shake-dice`), which also contains a `website/` folder; keep one canonical copy.
- No external trackers or CDNs.
- Preview with `python3 -m http.server`.
