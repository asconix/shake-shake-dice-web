Why you’ll love it

Shake to roll

Just shake your phone. The dice rattle while you shake and land the moment you stop. Prefer tapping? There’s a button too.

Real physics

Every die is simulated for real: it bounces off the table edge, knocks into other dice and settles with a natural result.

Gem-style dice

Glossy, colorful dice on a pool-table-style felt. Pick the color you like best.

Sound that feels right

Rattling, clacking and tumbling sounds make every roll satisfying.
